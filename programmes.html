Questions

Good to know

How do I enrol?

Send us a WhatsApp message on +234 806 853 9497 with the programme and date you want. Our team confirms your place and guides you through the next steps.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Just enter your name, phone number and email. We set up your Hairlux account for you and email a link to set a password, so you can follow your training, attendance and certificate.

When can I start an online course?

As soon as your enrolment is confirmed. Sign in to the Academy with the email you used and the course is waiting in My Courses.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes, when you complete the programme and meet its attendance and assessment requirements. Every certificate can be checked on our certificate verification page.

What if I cannot attend?

Contact us as early as possible. Changes and refunds follow our refund policy.
