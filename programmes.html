Questions

Good to know

How does the deposit work?

For in-branch training you can pay part of the fee to secure your seat. You see the exact amount before you pay. The balance is paid before your class starts.

How do I pay the balance?

Online with the "Pay my balance" link in your confirmation email, from your Academy account, or at the branch before the class. We remind you by email before the start date. The class cannot be started with a balance outstanding.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Just enter your name, phone number and email. We set up your Hairlux account for you and email a link to set a password, so you can follow your training, attendance and certificate.

When can I start an online course?

Straight after payment. Sign in to the Academy with the email you used and the course is waiting in My Courses.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes, when you complete the programme and meet its attendance and assessment requirements. Every certificate can be checked on our certificate verification page.

What if I cannot attend?

Contact us as early as possible. Changes and refunds follow our refund policy.
